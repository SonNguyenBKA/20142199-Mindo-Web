# Peer: Implementation Plan

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-peer-design.md`
**Verify:** each task ends with `tsc` and `lint`. At the end, run `build`, then check in the browser with `agent@local.test`: buy 1 Peer, then look at the list, the detail page and the Đại lý tab on desktop and mobile.

1. **BFF, types, service.**
   - Whitelist `GET agency/packages/config` and `POST agency/packages`.
   - Add `src/types/peer.ts` and `src/services/peer.service.ts`.
2. **Domain** (`src/lib/peer-pricing.ts`):
   - `pricePackages(totalBefore, qty, config)` returns segments, gross/net USD, net VND and the attained tier.
   - `expandPeers(packages)` turns packages into individual Peers.
   - Formatters `formatUsd` and `formatPeerCode`.
3. **Shared UI:**
   - `PeerTabs` (URL-driven, reusing SegmentedControl if it fits).
   - `PeerArtwork`, `SummaryRow`, `ResultCard`.
   - Tier badges come from `AGENCY_TIERS`.
4. **Sở hữu Peer tab:** quantity stepper, tier cards, next-tier hint, summary, gate, insufficient balance, buy mutation, success and failure results.
5. **Peer của tôi tab:** search, grid, pagination, empty state. Plus the `/peer/[number]` detail page with Thuộc tính and Quyền lợi tabs.
6. **Đại lý tab:** top pill, downline block, commission table and mobile list, month picker, detail drawer or sheet, "Hoa hồng của bạn" card, and the reused `ReferralCard`.
7. **Page wiring:**
   - `/peer` page with its mobile header.
   - `/peer/[number]` page.
   - `pageTitle` for the detail page.
8. **Verify.**
